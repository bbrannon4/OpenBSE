MODELING NOTES FOR ASHRAE STANDARD 140 


===============================================================================
INTRODUCTION
===============================================================================
This document shall include supplemental information about the ASHRAE Standard 
140 tests performed.  One S140outNotes document shall be provided for each 
set of tests (e.g., one for the building thermal and fabric load tests of 
Section 7.2; one for the space cooling equipment analytical 
verification tests of Sections 9.2; etc.)  The types of information 
listed below shall be provided in this document, each in a separate section:

a. Software information
b. Alternative modeling methods
c. Equivalent modeling methods
d. Nonspecified inputs
e. Omitted test cases and results
f. Changes made to source code for the purpose of running the tests, where
   such changes are not available in publicly released versions of the software
g. Anomalous results.

Notes in this document shall be limited to the topics shown above.  Notes must 
be factual and objective and shall only refer to the software being tested.  
Notes shall not refer to any other software program.

INFORMATIVE NOTE: Text at the start of each section describes the content of 
the section for the reader and provides instructions for supplying the content.  
Sample notes are provided in a separate document (S140outNotes_Examples.TXT).

===============================================================================
CONTENT: If the software being tested provides alternative modeling methods or

INSTRUCTIONS:  If alternative modeling methods are applicable, a separate note

The standard format shown below and a separate number and title for each note
shall be applied.  If alternative modeling methods are not applicable, specify
"NONE" in place of the information below.


NOTE 1 - Part-load operation of the packaged unit
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    How the unit meets a load below its capacity within a time step.

1.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):
    proportional: the unit runs for the part-load fraction of the time step
        needed to meet the load; energy follows the part-load ratio with the
        cycling degradation curve given in the input.
    on_off: the unit runs for the whole time step whenever the zone calls.

1.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    proportional, with cycling degradation coefficient 0.229
        (CDF = 1 - 0.229 (1 - PLR)), in every case.


NOTE 2 - DX coil sensible/latent split
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Division of the DX coil's total capacity into sensible and latent parts,
    including dry-coil operation.

2.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):
    autocalculate_shr true (default): the sensible heat ratio is computed
        each time step by the apparatus dew point / bypass factor method, with
        the bypass factor calibrated at the rated point (rated capacity, SHR
        and airflow); a dry coil gives SHR = 1.
    autocalculate_shr false: the rated SHR is applied at all conditions.
    dry_coil_wet_bulb_iteration true (default; with autocalculate_shr): when
        the coil is dry, capacity and EIR are read at the entering wet-bulb at
        which the coil would just be dry, not at the actual wet-bulb.
    dry_coil_wet_bulb_iteration false: dry-coil capacity is the capacity at
        the actual wet-bulb scaled by its sensible fraction.

2.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    autocalculate_shr and dry_coil_wet_bulb_iteration, both true, in
        every case.


NOTE 3 - Simulation time step
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Length of the calculation time step for the zone heat balance and HVAC.

3.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):
    1 to 60 time steps per hour (`simulation: timesteps_per_hour`).
        Physical Meaning: the heat balance, controls and equipment are solved
        this many times per hour; weather is interpolated within the hour.

3.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    1 time step per hour in every case.

NOTE 1 - Performance map form
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    DX unit capacity and power as functions of entering and outdoor conditions.

1.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    9.2.1.10 and Tables 9-6 (CE100MAP and the case maps)

1.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    Total capacity and EIR entered as tables on entering wet-bulb x outdoor
    dry-bulb, taken from the gross (fan heat excluded) data of the performance
    map; the sensible capacity's dependence on entering dry-bulb is computed by
    the apparatus dew point / bypass factor model.

1.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    In the map, total capacity and power depend on entering wet-bulb and
    outdoor dry-bulb only, and the sensible capacity's dependence on entering
    dry-bulb is the bypass relation the program computes. The program has no
    three-way table input, and 9.2.1.10.3 directs the use of the closest
    match where the program's input form differs from the map.
